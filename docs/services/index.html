File help and prototyping

How we work

Send a DXF, SVG, AI or PDF drawn at 1:1 in millimetres, or a sketch and the sizes you need. We check the file for kerf, tabs and nesting, quote within 24 hours, and cut once you approve. Most jobs are ready in 3 to 5 working days. Collect from Coomera or we post anywhere in Australia.

What we do not do

We do not cut metal of any kind: no steel, stainless, aluminium, brass or copper. We also do not cut PVC, chlorinated vinyl, fibreglass, ABS or polycarbonate over 3 mm, because they are unsafe to laser or damage the machine. For those jobs we will happily point you to a local metal cutter or router shop.
