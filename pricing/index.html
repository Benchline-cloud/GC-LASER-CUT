How a quote is worked out

Every quote is machine time plus material plus any setup. Machine time is what the laser actually spends cutting or engraving your file, so a simple outline in 3 mm MDF is quick and cheap, while a dense engraving or thick acrylic takes longer. We nest parts tightly to keep sheet waste down, and we tell you before starting if a change to the design would make it cheaper.

Material is quoted separately at cost, or bring your own sheet as long as it is laser-safe. There is no minimum quantity. Shipping is at cost and confirmed in the quote.
