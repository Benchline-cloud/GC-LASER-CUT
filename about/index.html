Our Story

GC Laser Cut started in Coomera in 2023 with one 130 W CO2 laser and a simple rule: do the materials that machine cuts best, and do them properly. That means timber, MDF, acrylic, PVC-free vinyl, leather and thin polycarbonate, and nothing else. We do not cut metal.
